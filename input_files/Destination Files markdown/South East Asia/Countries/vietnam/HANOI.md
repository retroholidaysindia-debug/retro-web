# 1. DESTINATION SUMMARY

Short description:

Hanoi is a timeless capital where 1000-year-old temples and French colonial boulevards weave through a buzzing maze of motorbikes and street markets. Blending rich heritage, soulful culture—from Hoan Kiem Lake to the Old Quarter—and world-famous street food like Pho and Egg Coffee, it offers a charming, affordable, and deeply authentic Southeast Asian escape.

travel_agent_summary:

Hanoi performs best when positioned as a soulful blend of ancient heritage, French colonial charm, and authentic street-level energy.
It’s not about “luxury speed” — it’s about slowing down, eating well, and soaking in 1000 years of history.

For first-time visitors, balance the must-see landmarks with neighbourhood wandering. Day formula: Morning at Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum + One Pillar Pagoda → Afternoon lost in Hanoi Old Quarter for 36 streets, temples, and coffee shops → Evening by Hoan Kiem Lake with the water puppet show.
Don’t try to rush. Hanoi rewards people who walk, sit, and observe.

For honeymooners and couples, Design around romance, quiet corners, and food.
Stay in the French Quarter for boutique hotels and tree-lined streets. Plan rooftop dinners with lake views, cyclo rides through the Old Quarter at dusk, and day trips to Halong Bay or Ninh Binh for that dreamy landscape.
Skip the packed group tours. Focus on 2-3 experiences per day with lots of cafe time.

For families, Location is key — stay in Old Quarter or near Hoan Kiem Lake so everything is walkable. Prioritise kid-friendly spots: Vietnam Museum of Ethnology, Hoan Kiem Lake + Ngoc Son Temple, West Lake for paddle boats.
Build in midday breaks — Hanoi heat + motorbikes can be tiring. Add a cooking class or street food tour so kids get involved. Ha-long Bay overnight cruise works great for families.

For budget travellers, Hanoi is one of Asia’s best value capitals. Stay: Hostels and boutique hotels in Old Quarter from $12-$25/night. Eat: World-class street food under $3 — Pho, Bun Cha, Banh Mi, Egg Coffee.
Do: 80% of Hanoi is free — walking Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem Lake, Temple of Literature, Train Street, and night markets. Transport: Use Grab bikes or walk. Day trips to Tam Coc or Ba Vi by bus are under $15.

# 6. IDEAL TRIP LENGTH

minimum_nights:
2-3

ideal_nights:
4–5

7

Travel-agent interpretation:

1-2 nights:
Rush trip: Old Quarter + Hoan Kiem Lake + 1 food tour. You’ll get the vibe but miss day trips. Good only for layovers.

3 nights:
Strong city-break format: Old Quarter + Water Puppet + Ho Chi Minh Complex + Temple of Literature + Train Street + Morning at West Lake + shopping.
No time for Ha-long Bay.

4 nights:
2 days Hanoi city + 1 day trip to Ninh Binh/Tam Coc + 1 buffer day for cafes and slow exploring. Perfect balance.

5 nights:
2 days Hanoi + 2D1N Ha-long Bay cruise + 1 day free for cooking class/spa/shopping. Tourists leave feeling they “saw Vietnam”.

10+ nights:
Recommend positioning this as a multi-stop Vietnam itinerary (e.g., Hanoi + Ha-long + Ninh Binh + Hue + Hoi An + Ho Chi Minh) rather than pure Hanoi.

# 10. BUDGET STRATEGY

Where money can be saved:

* Travel in shoulder seasons 
* Stay in Old Quarter or Ba Dinh, not lakefront 5★ – 3★ boutique hotels
* Use Grab Bike + Walk as primary mode
* Mix paid attractions with free attractions
* Rely on street food + bia hoi + local cafes
* Book Halong Bay 2D1N group cruise, not private yacht
* Choose cooking class + market tour bundle

Where saving money is usually NOT recommended:

* Extremely cheap hostels outside Old Quarter 
* Ignoring reviews for “$5 hotels”
* Skipping Ha Long Bay to save $100
* Cheapest night bus/flight to save 3 hours 
* Cutting costs with no airport transfer
* Avoiding all tours and doing everything solo

Travel-agent rule:

A slightly more expensive but centrally located, well-reviewed 3★ hotel in Old Quarter generally delivers better total value than a cheaper hotel 5km away. You save 2 hrs daily in traffic + $10 in Grab + you can walk back for afternoon rest. In Hanoi, location = time = money.

# 13. GETTING AROUND

Primary modes:

* WALKING
* GRAB BIKE / GRAB CAR
* TAXI 
* PUBLIC BUS
* PRIVATE_DRIVER
* CYCLO

Travel-agent interpretation:

Default recommendation: WALKING + GRAB as the primary mode; Hanoi Old Quarter is compact, and 80% of attractions are within 2km. Frame it as “walkable, chaotic, and full of life”.

Position PUBLIC BUS as the budget experience; Bus 86 goes Airport ↔ Old Quarter. Safe, but crowded and slow. Good for backpackers and daytime only. Use Google Maps + Moovit app.

Position TAXI/GRAB as comfort upgrades
Use for airport transfers, late nights after 10 PM, rain, seniors, and door-to-door hotel access. Avoid street meter taxis. Always use the Grab app or Mai Linh / Vinasun to avoid scams.

Highlight PRIVATE_DRIVER for day trips
Book for Ninh Binh, Ha Long Bay, Sapa. $45-$60/day for car. Much better value than piecing together buses. Driver waits, handles luggage, and saves time.

Position CYCLO as an experience, not transport
Ride in Old Quarter. Tourist thing only. Negotiate price first. Not practical, but great for photos.

* Groups by geography: Day 1 City, Day 2-3 Ha Long, Day 4 Day trip
* Uses hotel location strategically: OLD QUARTER for first timers, BA DINH for luxury
* Places outdoor early morning 8-11 AM or after 4 PM
