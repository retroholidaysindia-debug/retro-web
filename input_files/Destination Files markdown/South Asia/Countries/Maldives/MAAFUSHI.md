# 1. DESTINATION SUMMARY

short_description:  
Maafushi is Maldives' #1 local island for budget travelers, 27km south of Male. 1.2km long island with guesthouses $60-$120, bikini beach, dive centers $50/dive, water sports, and daily tours to sandbank, snorkeling, dolphin, resort day visit. No overwater villa, no alcohol on island, but floating bar boat + day trips give Maldives experience at 70% less cost. Perfect for backpackers, divers on budget, and travelers wanting Maldives + local life.

travel_agent_summary:  
Maafushi works best as 2-3 nights budget Maldives alternative to private resort, or 1N add-on before/after resort.

For budget travelers, Maafushi is best value - Guesthouse $70 + tours $35-$50 + diving $50 + public ferry $3. Total $100-$150/day vs resort $500+.

For families on budget, choose sea-view guesthouse with AC + bikini beach + sandbank tour shallow + dolphin cruise. All activities same as resort but cheaper.

For a honeymoon on budget, Kaani Grand Seaview or Arena Beach Hotel with private beach area + floating breakfast + a private dinner on a floating bar. Not overwater but decent.

For divers, Maafushi has 10+ dive centres, PADI courses $350-$450, manta/whale shark day trips to South Male/Ari, night shark snorkelling.

For backpackers, Maafushi is a social hub - Hostels + cafes + tour shops every 20m + ferry to other local islands Gulhi, Guraidhoo, Fulidhoo.

Not for an ultra-luxury honeymoon expecting a private pool overwater villa - That is a resort island only.

# 10. BUDGET STRATEGY

Where money can be saved:  
- Public ferry Male→Maafushi $3 1.5hr vs speedboat $25 45min - Save $22 but slow + crowded
- Guesthouse with free snorkel gear + kayak vs rental
- Group tours $35-$50 vs private $200-$400 - Share cost
- Eat at local cafes $5-$10 vs guesthouse $15
- Book 3 tours combo - Sandbank + snorkeling + dolphin - $100 vs separate $150
- Travel May-Sep - Guesthouse 20-30% off

Where saving money is usually NOT recommended:  
- Cheapest guesthouse with no AC + bad bathroom in summer - Hot + humid + mosquitoes
- Public ferry with big luggage + toddler - Bumpy + crowded + no AC + long
- Cutting travel insurance - Speedboat weather cancellation + dive safety
- No reef shoes - Coral cuts
- Cheap dive center with no PADI + old gear - Safety risk

Travel-agent rule:  
Maafushi total budget = Guesthouse $70-$120 + meals $20-$30 + 1 tour $40 + ferry $25 = $155-$215/day per room. Vs resort $500-$1500/day. Best value in Maldives. But no overwater villa + no alcohol on island. If client wants overwater, sell 1N Maafushi + 2N resort split.

# 11. ARRIVAL INFORMATION

Primary airport: Velana MLE - 27km north of Maafushi  
No airport in Maafushi

From MLE to Maafushi:  
- Public ferry: MLE → Male ferry $1 10min + Male → Maafushi ferry #309 1.5hr $3. Departs Male 3 PM daily except Fri. Maafushi→Male 7:30 AM.
- Speedboat: Direct MLE→Maafushi or Male→Maafushi 45-60min $25-$35 pp one way. Many operators - iCom, Maafushi Tours. Runs 8 AM-6 PM every 1-2hr. Private speedboat $150-$200 per boat.
- No seaplane to Maafushi

From Maafushi to other local islands:  
- Gulhi 20min $5 ferry
- Guraidhoo 30min
- Fulidhoo 1.5hr
