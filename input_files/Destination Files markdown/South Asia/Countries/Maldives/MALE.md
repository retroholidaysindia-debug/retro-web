# 1. DESTINATION SUMMARY

short_description:  
Maldives is 1,192 coral islands in the Indian Ocean, famous for overwater villas, white sand, turquoise lagoon, house reef snorkelling, diving with manta + whale sharks. Male is a small capital island 2km long; Hulhumale airport island MLE is the main international gateway. Perfect for honeymoon, luxury, diving, family beach. Not a cultural city - Pure beach + water.

travel_agent_summary:  
Maldives works best as 3-5 nights beach + water villa finale after Sri Lanka/Dubai/Bali or standalone honeymoon.

For first-time visitors, balance a quick Male city tour + resort overwater villa + sandbank trip + dolphin sunset + snorkelling house reef.

For honeymooners, focus on a private overwater villa with pool, sunset dolphin cruise, private sandbank dinner, spa, and no Male stay - Direct to resort.

For families, choose a big island resort with kid’s club + shallow lagoon + house reef + all-inclusive - Kurumba, Sun Siyam, Centara, Hard Rock.

For luxury, stay at Soneva Fushi, Cheval Blanc Randheli, One&Only Reethi Rah, Velaa Private Island - ultra-luxury + private pool + butler.

For budget, stay at a Maafushi / Hulhumale guesthouse $60-$120 + local island tours + public ferry to save speedboat cost.

For divers, liveaboard or dive resort in Ari Atoll, Baa Atoll (Manta season May-Nov), South Male Atoll.

# 10. BUDGET STRATEGY

Where money can be saved:  
- Travel May-Sep low season - 30-50% off + free seaplane offers
- Choose Maafushi / Dhigurah guesthouse $70-$120 + local tours $40 vs resort $500+
- Choose speedboat resort (close to Male 30min) vs seaplane resort (far) to save $400 pp seaplane
- All-Inclusive vs Half Board - AI saves if family + drinks + activities
- 3N instead of 5N + combine with Sri Lanka/Dubai

Where saving money is usually NOT recommended:  
- Very cheap guesthouse far + public ferry only - Waste full day in transfers
- Cutting seaplane luggage limit - Extra charge $5/kg
- No snorkel gear - Rent low quality. Buy mask
- Bad overwater villa old + no pool + no house reef - Main experience ruined
- No travel insurance - Speedboat/seaplane weather cancellation

Travel-agent rule:  
Maldives budget = $150/day guesthouse + $400/day 4★ + $800-$2000/day 5★ overwater. Overwater villa with pool is #1 must. Speedboat 30-60min is best value for family. Seaplane for honeymoon luxury + far atolls best reef. Always include green tax $6/person/night + transfer cost.

# 11. ARRIVAL INFORMATION

Primary airport: Velana International Airport MLE - Hulhule Island  
Male city: 10min boat from airport  
Hulhumale town: 20min bus/taxi from airport via bridge

From Airport to Resort:  
- Speedboat: 15-60min for North/South Male Atoll resorts - $150-$350 RT per person, resort arranges. Shared.
- Seaplane: 30-60min for Ari, Baa, Lhaviyani far atolls - $400-$800 RT per person. Trans Maldivian Airways. 15kg luggage limit.
- Domestic flight + speedboat: For far atolls Addu, Fuvahmulah - $200-$400 + speedboat

From Airport to Male city: 10min public ferry $1 + private speedboat $10

From India/Neighbor: Direct flights from DEL, BOM, BLR, COK, TRV - 2-4hr to MLE.

# 12. AIRPORT TRANSFER LOGIC

## Family
Speedboat resort 30-60min + hotel welcome + life jacket for kids. Avoid seaplane with toddlers under 2. Resort buggy + kids club. All-inclusive recommended.

## Honeymoon  
Private speedboat or seaplane with champagne + flower + overwater villa private pool + direct check-in to villa. No Male stay - Direct to resort from MLE.

## Luxury  
Private luxury yacht or seaplane private charter + private island butler pickup + VIP lounge at MLE seaplane terminal. Cheval Blanc, Soneva Fushi private lounge.

## Budget  
Public ferry to Maafushi $3 + guesthouse pickup $5. Or Hulhumale guesthouse $10 taxi from MLE. Local tours arranged by guesthouse.

## Senior  
Speedboat resort 30min max - Less bumpy. Avoid seaplane if mobility issue (steps to plane + water landing). Resort with elevator? Overwater villas have steps. Choose beach villa ground floor for senior.

# 13. GETTING AROUND

Primary modes:
- RESORT BUGGY for big islands
- SPEEDBOAT for close resorts + excursions
- SEAPLANE for far atolls + scenic
- WALKING inside resort - Sandy paths
- BICYCLE in some big resorts - Kurumba, Sun Siyam

Travel-agent interpretation:  
Maldives transport = transfers between islands only. Inside Male city taxi $2-$3, ferry $1. Inside resort walking/buggy. Excursions by Dhoni boat. No cars in resorts except buggy. No tuk-tuk. Seaplane only daytime 6 AM-4 PM. Speedboat 24hr but bumpy at night.
