# 1. DESTINATION SUMMARY

Short description:

Colombo is Sri Lanka’s commercial capital and busiest city. Famous for colonial forts, skyscrapers, shopping malls, museums, street food, and seaside promenades. A mix of old + new — Dutch buildings next to Lotus Tower, tuk-tuks next to BMWs. Perfect for 1-2 nights for shopping, culture, food, and as a gateway.

travel_agent_summary:  
Colombo works best as a 1-2-night arrival/departure stop or city break between beach/hill country.

For first-time visitors, balance Gangaramaya Temple + Galle Face Green + Fort + National Museum + Pettah Market.

For honeymooners, focus on rooftop bars, luxury hotels, and fine dining.

For families, the National Museum, Viharamahadevi Park, and malls with kids’ zones are highlights.

For culture + food travellers, add Dutch Hospital, Independence Square, and street food tours.

For business travellers, Colombo has 5★ hotels, meeting venues, and best connectivity.

For budget travellers, Colombo offers hostels from $15, $1 buses, $2 street food, and free parks.

# 10. BUDGET STRATEGY

Where money can be saved:  
- Stay in guesthouses in Kollupitiya/Cinnamon Gardens vs 5★ Fort
- Eat at street food + local rice & curry $2-$4
- Use bus + train + tuk-tuk vs private car
- Free activities: Galle Face, Parks, Temples, Walking tours
- Shop at Pettah Market vs malls

Where saving money is usually NOT recommended:  
- Bad area location — far from attractions = taxi costs
- Cutting AC hotel — heat + humidity is intense
- Cheap taxis at night — use Uber/Pick Me

Travel-agent rule:  
1 night in a good central hotel beats 3 nights commuting. You pay for location to see Colombo without traffic stress.

# 13. GETTING AROUND

Primary modes:
- TUK-TUK for short distances within 3km
- TAXI / UBER / PICKME for AC travel across the city
- WALKING in Fort, Galle Face, Dutch Hospital areas
- TRAIN for suburbs + day trips

Travel-agent interpretation:  
Colombo is spread out, and traffic is heavy 8-10 AM, 5-8 PM. 20min can become 1hr. Hot + humid 28-32°C. Book a private car with AC for full-day tours. Tuk-tuks $1-$3 within area. Walking only in Fort/Galle Face early morning. Use Uber for safety at night.

# 32. TRAVEL AGENT INSIGHTS

#### What inexperienced planners often do wrong
- Stay more than 2 nights - City gets tiring
- Book hotel in Pettah to save money - Traffic + noise
- Try to walk everywhere midday - Too hot + humid
- Skip Galle Face at sunset - Miss #1 free experience
- Use local buses with luggage - Not tourist-friendly

#### What an experienced planner does differently
1. Starts with purpose  
   - Family = Museum + Mall + Park  
   - Honeymoon = 5★ Hotel + Rooftop + 1N only  
   - Business = Fort hotel + Meeting rooms

2. Groups by geography: Day 1 Fort/Pettah, Day 2 Galle Face/Museum

3. Uses hotel location strategically: GALLE_FACE for convenience

4. Places Colombo at start or end - Gateway city

5. Treats traffic seriously - 1 activity per half day max

6. Always use AC transport - Heat + humidity
