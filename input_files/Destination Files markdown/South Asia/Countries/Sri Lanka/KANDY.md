# 1. DESTINATION SUMMARY

Short description:

Kandy is Sri Lanka’s cultural capital and last kingdom. Set around a sacred lake and hills, it’s home to the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic. Expect colonial charm, Buddhist temples, botanical gardens, tea culture, and traditional dance — perfect for heritage, spirituality, and cool-climate relaxation.

Travel agent summary:

Kandy works best as a 2-3 night cultural + nature stop between Dambulla and Nuwara Eliya/Ella.

For first-time visitors, balance Temple of the Tooth + Kandy Lake + Peradeniya Botanical Gardens + cultural show.

For culture + spirituality travellers, focus on Temple of the Tooth ceremonies, Buddhist sites, and Royal Palace complex.

For families, the Botanical Gardens, Uda Watta Kele Sanctuary, and train ride to Nuwara Eliya are highlights. Cool weather is a big plus.

For honeymooners, add boutique hotels with lake/tea estate views, spa, and scenic train.

For budget travellers, Kandy offers guesthouses from $20, free lake walks, and $5 temples. Food and transport are cheap.

# 10. BUDGET STRATEGY

Where money can be saved:

* Stay in guesthouses near the lake
* Eat at local rice & curry spots
* Use tuk-tuk + local bus
* Free activities: lake walk, market, Uda Watta Kele
* Book train tickets early

Where saving money is usually NOT recommended:

* Temple guide — context matters
* Bad hotel location — hills = tiring walks
* Cutting the cultural show — it’s iconic

Travel-agent rule:
A lake-view 3★ hotel beats a cheaper hilltop homestay. You’ll walk less and enjoy evenings more.

#### What inexperienced planners often do wrong
- Try to fit Sigiriya + Dambulla + Kandy + Nuwara Eliya in 2 nights - Clients end up in car 5hrs/day with zero rest
- Book cheap hilltop homestay 2km from lake to "save $15" - Client spends all time climbing and misses evening lake walks
- Plan Botanical Gardens + Uda Watta Kele + Temple + Train on same day - Too much walking. Clients burn out
- Combine Temple puja + Cultural Show + Market + Gardens on Day 1 - Impossible. Temple needs 2-3hr
- Overlook Temple dress code - No shorts/shoulders. Clients get turned away
- Forget to book train tickets 30 days ahead - Sold out in peak season
- Sell Kandy as "7N beach base" - Clients get bored. Kandy is culture, not beach
- Ignore Perahera season Jul-Aug - Hotels 3x price and fully booked 6 months ahead

#### What an experienced planner does differently

1. Starts with purpose  
   - Family = Lake hotel + Gardens + 1 train ride + Pool time  
   - Honeymoon = Lake/Tea view boutique + Spa + 1 activity/day  
   - Budget = Guesthouse + Tuk-tuks + Free lake walk + Local food

2. Groups by geography: Day 1 City + Temple, Day 2 Gardens + West, Day 3 Train/Day trip

3. Uses hotel location strategically: LAKE VIEW for first timers, HANTHANA HILLS for honeymoon, PERADENIYA for quiet

4. Places outdoor early morning 7-10 AM or after 4 PM - Avoid 12-3 PM sun in Gardens

5. Treats train ride as a full-day commitment - 7:30 AM pickup, 5:30 PM return

6. Chooses 2-3 high-value experiences over 8 checklist items - 1 puja, 1 garden, 1 train > rushing 8 spots

7. Always builds 1 buffer day - For rain, traffic, or just lake + spa time
