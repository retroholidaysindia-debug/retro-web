# 1. DESTINATION SUMMARY

Short description:

Mirissa is Sri Lanka’s #1 beach + whale watching town. Famous for crescent-shaped palm beach, golden sunsets, surf, turtle Snorkelling, and blue whale sightings Nov-Apr. Laid-back backpacker vibe by day, chilled beach bars by night — perfect for relaxation, romance, and ocean adventures.

Travel agent summary:

Mirissa works best as a 2-3-night beach + wildlife stop between Galle and Yala/Tangalle.

For first-time visitors, balance Whale Watching + Mirissa Beach + Parrot Rock + Coconut Tree Hill.

For honeymooners, focus on beachfront boutique hotels, sunset dinners, and private boat tours.

For families, the calm bay, turtle snorkelling, and Secret Beach are highlights. Surf lessons for teens.

For nature + photography travellers, add whale watching, stilt fishermen, and sunrise at Coconut Tree Hill.

For budget travellers, Mirissa offers guesthouses from $20, $2 tuk-tuks, $50 whale watching, and cheap beach food. Food and transport are affordable.

# 10. BUDGET STRATEGY

Where money can be saved:  
- Stay in guesthouses inland vs beachfront
- Eat at local rice & curry + beach shacks $3-$5
- Use tuk-tuk + local bus
- Free activities: Beach, Coconut Tree Hill, Parrot Rock, sunset
- Book whale watching in group $40-$50

Where saving money is usually NOT recommended:  
- Bad hotel location — far from beach = tuk-tuk costs
- Cutting whale watching — it’s the #1 highlight Nov-Apr
- Cheap boat operators — safety matters

Travel-agent rule:  
A beachfront hotel for 2 nights beats 4 nights inland. You get the sunset + ocean sound that makes Mirissa special.

# 32. TRAVEL AGENT INSIGHTS

#### What inexperienced planners often do wrong
- Skip whale watching to "sleep in" - Miss the #1 highlight Nov-Apr
- Book hotel far from beach - Wastes time + tuk-tuk cost
- Visit Jun-Aug - Rough sea, no whales
- Try to do Galle + Yala + Whales in 1 day - Impossible
- Plan beach all day without shade - Sunburn risk

#### What an experienced planner does differently
1. Starts with purpose  
   - Family = Beach + Turtles + Galle  
   - Honeymoon = Beach villa + Whales + Sunset  
   - Budget = Guesthouse + Group whale tour

2. Groups by geography: Day 1 Beach, Day 2 Whales + Ocean, Day 3 Day trip

3. Uses hotel location strategically: BEACHFRONT for convenience, HILL for views

4. Places whale watching early 6-10 AM - Calm sea, more sightings

5. Treats season seriously - Nov-Apr only for whales

6. Always book whale tour in advance - Boats fill up Dec-Feb
