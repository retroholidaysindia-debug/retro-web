# 1. DESTINATION SUMMARY

short_description:  
Thimphu is Bhutan’s charming capital in a high Himalayan valley at 2,334m. No traffic lights. Famous for Tashichho Dzong, Buddha Dordenma, Memorial Chorten, folk heritage, the textile museum, the weekend market, and a Gross National Happiness vibe. Clean, calm, colourful prayer flags - perfect 2-3 nights for culture, spirituality, and Himalayan calm before Punakha/Paro.

travel_agent_summary:  
Thimphu works best as a 2-3 night cultural + spiritual base at the start of a Bhutan trip. All tours need a licensed Bhutanese guide + SDF.

For first-time visitors, balance Buddha Dordenma + Tashichho Dzong + Memorial Chorten + Folk Heritage Museum + Weekend Market.

For culture lovers, add National Textile Museum, Motithang Takin Preserve, Changangkha Lhakhang, and Simply Bhutan museum.

For families, the Buddha Point, Takin Zoo, weekend market, and Changlimithang archery ground are highlights.

For honeymooners, focus on private dzong evening view, luxury lodges like Le Meridien/Terma Linca, spa, and cafe culture in town.

For luxury travellers, Bhutan is high-value, low-impact - All 3★ minimum mandated, upgrade to 5★ Le Meridien, Six Senses Thimphu, Pemako.

For budget travellers, note Bhutan has SDF $100/day + guide/transport mandatory - Not cheap like Nepal. But food + stay included in package.

# 10. BUDGET STRATEGY

Where money can be saved:  
- Travel in low season Dec-Feb, Jun-Aug - SDF still same but hotel lower
- Choose 3★ govt approved hotels vs 5★ Six Senses
- Group tour (shared guide/car) vs private
- Shop at weekend market vs boutique for small souvenirs
- Thimphu as 2N not 4N to save SDF days

Where saving money is usually NOT recommended:  
- Unlicensed guide/car - Illegal in Bhutan. Must be licensed
- Cutting SDF - Mandatory $100/person/day. Paid to govt
- Cheap textiles - Fake imports. Buy from govt Handicrafts Emporium
- No guide at Dzong - Mandatory and story needed

Travel-agent rule:  
Bhutan is not Nepal. Client must budget $250-$350 per person per day minimum (SDF $100 + 3★ hotel + guide + car + meals). Thimphu is best as part of a 5-6N Bhutan circuit: Thimphu 2N + Punakha 1N + Paro 2N.
