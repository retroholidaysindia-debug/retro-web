# 1. DESTINATION SUMMARY

short_description:
Kathmandu is Nepal’s chaotic, spiritual, heritage capital in Kathmandu Valley. Famous for 7 UNESCO World Heritage Sites, Swayambhu Monkey Temple, Pashupatinath, Boudhanath Stupa, Durbar Squares, Thamel markets, and as gateway to Everest + Annapurna. A mix of ancient temples, Buddhist stupas, busy bazaars, and Himalayan vibe — perfect for 2-3 nights culture + history + shopping.

travel_agent_summary:  
Kathmandu works best as a 2-3 night cultural base at start or end of Nepal trip.

For first-time visitors, balance Pashupatinath + Boudhanath + Swayambhu + Kathmandu Durbar Square + Thamel walk.

For pilgrims, focus on Pashupatinath darshan, Boudhanath kora, Swayambhu, and a day trip to Dakshinkali + Budhanilkantha.

For families, the Swayambhu monkey temple, Garden of Dreams, Durbar Square, and shopping in Thamel are highlights.

For culture + photography, add Patan Durbar Square, Bhaktapur Durbar Square, and early morning aarti at Pashupatinath.

For budget travellers, Kathmandu offers hostels from $10, MoMos $1-$2, $5 temple entry, and cheap Thamel stays. Excellent value for heritage.

# 10. BUDGET STRATEGY

Where money can be saved:  
- Stay in Thamel guesthouses $15-$35 vs 5★ $150+
- Eat at local Newari + Thakali + momo $2-$5
- Use shared heritage day tour $15-$20 vs private $50-$80
- Free: Boudhanath kora, Thamel walk, Garden of Dreams outside
- Shop at Asan bazaar for local prices vs Thamel tourist prices

Where saving money is usually NOT recommended:  
- Very cheap unlicensed guide - Wrong history + no entry help
- Cutting private car - Traffic + dust + walking too much
- Bad location far from Thamel - Transport + time waste
- No guide at Pashupatinath - Miss aarti timing + rituals

Travel-agent rule:  
A Thamel hotel for 3 nights beats 5 nights far away. Walk to shops + restaurants + tour pickup. Private car half-day for UNESCO sites saves 2hrs.

#### What inexperienced planners often do wrong
- Try to cover 4 UNESCO sites + Bhaktapur + Nagarkot in 1 day - Too rushed + traffic
- Book hotel far from Thamel to save $10 - Lose time in traffic 1hr each side
- Plan Pashupatinath midday - Miss morning/evening aarti + very hot + cremation smoke
- Forget temple entry fees + camera fees separate - Budget wrong
- Plan Thamel walking at midday dust - Morning/evening better

#### What an experienced planner does differently
1. Starts with purpose  
   - Pilgrim = Pashupatinath + Boudha + Morning aarti  
   - Family = Swayambhu + Garden of Dreams + Easy temples  
   - Culture = Patan + Bhaktapur + Museum

2. Groups by geography: Thamel/Durbar together, Boudha/Pashupati together, Patan/Bhaktapur together.

3. Uses hotel location strategically: THAMEL or DURBAR MARG for walkability. Boudha for pilgrims.

4. Places outdoor temples early morning or after 4 PM. Dust + heat management.

5. Treats Boudha + Pashupati as half-day sacred cluster. Not rush.

6. Chooses smaller number of high-value experiences + shopping time. Don't checklist all 7 UNESCO in 2 nights.
