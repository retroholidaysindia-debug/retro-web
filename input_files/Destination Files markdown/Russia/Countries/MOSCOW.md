# 10. BUDGET STRATEGY

Where money can be saved:
- Stay near metro Komsomolskaya/Belorusskaya business hotel vs Red Square luxury - Save 40% + metro 5min to Red Square
- Eat at Teremok blini $3 + Mu-Mu cafeteria $6 + Stolovaya No57 GUM $6-$10 vs White Rabbit fine dining $60-$100
- Use metro day pass $2.5 vs taxi $10-$15 - Metro covers all + palace stations free museum
- Use free walking tour Red Square + museum free days + Gorky Park free + Zaryadye free + Izmailovo Market browsing free
- Travel Nov-Mar except New Year - Hotels 20-30% cheaper + less queue but cold -10°C

Where saving money is usually NOT recommended:
- Cheap hotel far from metro ring - Waste time + taxi cost + Moscow big 12.5M + traffic 2hrs
- No Bolshoi ticket advance - Sold out + expensive resale - Book 1-2 months early + $30-$150
- No Kremlin + Armoury ticket advance peak - Queue 1-2hrs without online ticket + Armoury slots limited
- No private guide for Kremlin + Armoury - History complex + guide worth $30-$40
- No Yandex taxi - Official taxi expensive + Yandex/Bolt $5-$10 best + app + cheap

Travel-agent rule:  
1 night in good central hotel near Okhotny Ryad/Kitay-Gorod beats 3 nights commuting from far. You pay for location to see Red Square + Kremlin without traffic + metro stress.
