# 1. DESTINATION SUMMARY

short_description:

Tahiti is the largest and most populated island of French Polynesia and the principal international gateway to The Islands of Tahiti. Formed from dramatic volcanic mountains in the South Pacific, the island combines the urban energy of Papeete with black-sand beaches, tropical valleys, waterfalls, archaeological sites, Polynesian culture, lagoon activities and some of the world's most important surfing landscapes.

The destination is anchored by Papeete Market, Place Vaiete and the capital waterfront, while the Museum of Tahiti and the Islands, Marae Arahurahu, Point Venus, Faarumai Waterfalls, Vaipahi Water Gardens, Mara'a Grottoes, Papenoo Valley and Teahupo'o provide deeper cultural, historical and natural experiences.

Tahiti's tourism identity extends far beyond its role as an airport gateway. Tahiti Nui contains Papeete, major resorts, museums, historic sites, black-sand beaches and the mountainous interior, while smaller Tahiti Iti forms the island's wilder peninsula and contains Teahupo'o, one of the world's most celebrated surf locations.

travel_agent_summary:

Tahiti performs best as a Polynesian-culture, nature, surfing, adventure, honeymoon, photography, gastronomy, gateway, family and tropical-island destination.

For first-time visitors, prioritize Papeete Market + Museum of Tahiti and the Islands + Point Venus + Faarumai Waterfalls + Vaipahi Water Gardens + Marae Arahurahu + Teahupo'o.

For families, combine beaches, waterfalls, botanical gardens, cultural museums, lagoon activities, easy nature experiences and Papeete rather than concentrating entirely on strenuous mountain adventures.

For honeymoon travelers, prioritize a lagoon-facing resort, Tahiti west-coast sunsets, Polynesian spa treatments, private island touring, fine dining, black-sand beaches and a Tahiti Iti excursion.

For adventure travelers, prioritize Teahupo'o, Papenoo Valley, hiking, waterfalls, surfing, diving, snorkeling, va'a paddling, whale watching in season and guided mountain experiences.

For repeat visitors, move beyond Papeete and the airport corridor into Tahiti Iti, Papenoo Valley, archaeological sites, mountain trails, remote waterfalls, east-coast landscapes and deeper Polynesian cultural experiences.

# 10. BUDGET STRATEGY

Where money can be saved:

* Stay in a Tahitian guesthouse or pension instead of a major resort.
* Use Papeete's roulottes for affordable evening meals.
* Shop at Papeete Market for local food and practical purchases.
* Use free beaches, viewpoints and selected natural attractions.
* Combine west-coast attractions into one rental-car day.
* Combine east-coast attractions into one island-tour day.
* Use buses around Tahiti when schedules and routing fit the itinerary.
* Reserve private excursions only for experiences where they materially improve access.

Where saving money is usually NOT recommended:

* Choosing an isolated property without calculating rental-car or taxi costs.
* Attempting remote valley or mountain exploration without suitable guidance.
* Booking unsuitable bargain transport for very early international departures.
* Cutting all marine activities when snorkeling, surfing, whale watching or lagoon experiences are primary trip goals.
* Treating Tahiti purely as an airport stop without allowing enough time to experience the island.

Travel-agent rule:

In Tahiti, **GUESTHOUSE_OR_WELL_LOCATED_HOTEL + RENTAL_CAR_CLUSTERING + ROULOTTES + SELECTIVE_PAID_EXCURSIONS** provides the strongest value.

For short stays, prioritize:

**PAPEETE + MUSEUM_OF_TAHITI + POINT_VENUS + WATERFALLS + WEST_COAST_CULTURE**.

For longer stays, add:

**TEAHUPOO + PAPENOO_VALLEY + VAIPAHI + MARAE_ARAHURAHU + HIKING + MARINE_ACTIVITIES**.

Operational note:

Excursion prices, museum admission, road conditions, surf access, boat tours, whale-watching availability and attraction operating conditions must be rechecked before travel.

* Treat Tahiti as interchangeable with THE_ISLANDS_OF_TAHITI even though Tahiti is one specific island within French Polynesia.
* Confuse TAHITI with BORA_BORA, MOOREA, TAHA'A or the entire Society Islands group.
* Include Bora Bora, Moorea and Raiatea attractions inside a Tahiti-only sightseeing inventory.
* Assume Tahiti's strongest experience is only a RESORT_STAY instead of recognizing CULTURE + VOLCANIC_NATURE + SURFING + WATERFALLS + BLACK_SAND_BEACHES + FOOD + ADVENTURE.
