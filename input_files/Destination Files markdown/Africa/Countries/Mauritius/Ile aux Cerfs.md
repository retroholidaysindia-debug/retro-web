# 10. BUDGET STRATEGY

- Choose shared boat transfers or package tours instead of private charters.
- Keep the island visit as a **day trip** or short 1-night add-on.
- Stay in a well-located Mauritius base area and transfer in for the excursion.
- Travel in the shoulder or rainy season for better package prices.
- Book meals and activities as part of a bundled tour when possible.

**Where saving money is not recommended:**

- Using unlicensed boats or unsafe operators.
- Skipping transfer planning just to save a small amount.
- Choosing poorly reviewed stays far from the departure jetty.
- Cutting corners on safety for snorkeling or water sports.

**Travel-agent rule:**

Île aux Cerfs offers the best value through **shared boat access, packaged day tours, and a short stay format**, while premium pricing is justified only for private transfers, exclusive beach setups, and luxury sea experiences.
